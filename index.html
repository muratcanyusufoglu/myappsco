Where we're focused

Three products in active development

These three get most of our time. Each one has a Supabase or Cloudflare backend, server-side subscription checks through RevenueCat, and AI features with a real cost per request.

HairOn

AI hairstyle try-on · iOS & Android

Upload a selfie and see more than 100 hairstyles and hair colors on your own face before you go to the salon.

  • AIImage editing models run behind 35 Supabase Edge Functions, with a server-side quota for each user
  • Reach31 languages · App Store and Google Play
  • Statusv1.0.22, released 7 Oct 2026

Milo

Reading journal · iOS & Android

Photograph a page and Milo keeps the sentence, together with the book, the date and the page number. Tap any word to see what it means in that sentence, whatever language the book is in.

  • AIContextual word definitions are moving to Claude In progress
  • Reach11 languages · books in any language
  • Statusv1.1.1, released 6 Oct 2026
View on the App Store →

Stirred

Home bar & cocktails · iOS

Mark the bottles on your shelf and Stirred shows what you can make tonight, plus the one bottle that would unlock the most new recipes. The library has 600 recipes, each checked against two independent published sources.

  • AIShelf scan: a photo of your bottles becomes inventory, through a Cloudflare Worker with schema-constrained output. UI localized with Claude Opus.
  • Reach31 languages
  • Statusv1.0, launched 9 Oct 2026
