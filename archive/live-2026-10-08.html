Why We Require Claude for Startups

Our studio is aggressively transitioning all generative logic to Anthropic's models due to superior structural adherence, deep multilingual reasoning, and low-latency JSON outputs essential for mobile Edge Functions.

1. Multilingual Contextual Parsing

Milo requires complex text correction. Claude's ability to natively understand structural nuances in Turkish, Japanese (Kanji breaks), and Korean (Hangul spacing) outperforms standard segmentation APIs[cite: 2].

2. Vision-to-Prompt Translation

For HairOn AI, Claude processes unstructured user desires ("I want a messy look suitable for an oval face") into precise parameters required by our backend GAN models[cite: 3].

3. Dynamic Localization at Edge

Replacing static translation JSON files with Claude-driven dynamic copy generation for personalized user experiences across 16 global markets.
